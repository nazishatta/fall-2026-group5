Fyrooz Anika Khan received her Bachelor's degree in Urban and Regional Planning from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology, Bangladesh, in 2022. She is currently pursuing her M.S. degree in Data Science at the George Washington University, Washington, DC, USA. She has prior research experience in transportation policy and planning, urban disaster management, sustainable urban development, land-use planning, and geospatial analysis. Her current research interests include generative AI, multi-agent systems, natural language processing and speech processing, deep learning, and computer vision, with applications in urban planning, transportation, healthcare, and environmental sustainability.
