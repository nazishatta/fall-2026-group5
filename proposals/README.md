# Proposals (`proposals/`)

The two proposals the project is built on.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`Project_proposal.md`](Project_proposal.md) | The project proposal from the course. Phase and step names in the whole repository (code, tests, docs, outputs and reports) follow it. |
| [`Group_proposal.md`](Group_proposal.md) | Our group's proposal: research questions, success criteria and thresholds, roles and plan. |

Both files were in `reports/` until 2026-11-17; `Group_proposal.md` was then called `Proposal.md`.
Some older documents still name the old paths, on purpose: the protocols
`src/v1_mnist/docs/p3_novelty_detection/held_out_class_novelty/HELD_OUT_CLASS_NOVELTY_PROTOCOL.md` and
`src/v1_mnist/docs/p4_guided_retraining/novelty_after_retraining/NOVELTY_AFTER_RETRAINING_PROTOCOL.md`
are fingerprinted (SHA-256) by their runs, so they are not edited, and the older rows of
`src/v1_mnist/docs/DEVIATIONS.md` describe the files as they were then. In all of them,
`reports/Proposal.md` means `proposals/Group_proposal.md`, and `reports/Project_proposal.md` means
`proposals/Project_proposal.md`.
